Published a detailed review paper on **Swarm Robotics and its Significance** at an IEEE conference. The paper surveys computational swarm intelligence algorithms and their practical application in multi-robot systems, highlighting decentralized, robust, and self-organized behaviors.

Key topics covered:

- Swarm intelligence fundamentals and biological inspiration
- Algorithm taxonomy: PSO, ACO, BEE algorithms, and more
- Simulation results and basic hardware validation
- Open challenges in real-world swarm deployment
